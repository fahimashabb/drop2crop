ESP32 Hardware Diagnostics

How to verify your device is working properly

🔴 1. Red Power LED (Board Power Check)

When you plug the 5V mobile charger into the ESP32, the red PWR LED on the board must turn on immediately and stay solid. If no LED lights up, verify your charger, USB cable, or power outlet.

📶 2. Wi-Fi "Proof of Life" Beacon

If the ESP32 is powered but cannot connect to your Wi-Fi within 12s, it automatically broadcasts an open Wi-Fi network named Drop2Crop_WiFi_Setup. If this appears in your phone's Wi-Fi list, the ESP32 microcontroller, CPU, and Wi-Fi chip are 100% healthy and working!

🌐 3. Live Dashboard "Device Online" Badge

Once connected to your home Wi-Fi and the cloud, the top status badge automatically changes from The Device is Not Online to a green pulsing Device Online. The telemetry cards update live every 2 seconds.

⚡ 4. Relay Mechanical "Click" Test

Switch to Mode 3 (Manual Cloud) and press "Turn ON Pump". You will hear an audible mechanical "CLICK" from the relay cube, and the channel 1 LED on the relay board will turn ON. You can also run the automated Diagnostic Pulse test from the controls card.

🔌 5. Recommended GPIO Ports & Relay Wiring

Connect your relay signal wires to the dedicated, clean GPIO pins (free from JTAG & Flash):

  • Relay IN1 (Clean Water Pump): Connect to GPIO 13 (or GPIO 21)
  • Relay IN2 (Fertigation Pump): Connect to GPIO 14 (or GPIO 47)
  • Relay VCC: Connect to 5Vin (5V rail) — NOT 3.3V!
  • Relay GND: Connect to ESP32 GND
  • Relay Blue Jumper: Keep attached across JD-VCC & VCC (or separate JD-VCC to 5V)
